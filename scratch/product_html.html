    Product Details

    Savor the authentic taste of Mix Chavanu, a crunchy and flavorful Gujarati namkeen snack handcrafted by Bhagvat Prasadam. Made from premium lentils, peanuts, and aromatic spices, this mixture namkeen delivers the perfect balance of taste and tradition.

    A timeless favorite among traditional Indian namkeen, our Mix Namkeen is ideal for Diwali snacks, festive gifting, or everyday tea-time delight. Each bite is a crunchy blend of flavors that celebrates authenticity and purity. Bring home this spicy Indian snack and enjoy a delicious part of India’s snacking heritage.
